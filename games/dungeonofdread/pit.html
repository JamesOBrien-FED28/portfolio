The Dungeon of Dread

You manage to overcome the troll by using your brain.

You offer him some of your bacon sandwich and as he goes to take it, you twat him over the head with a rock.

You pass through some winding passageways and eventually find a room. The floor is so far down that you cant see it.

There is a series of dodgy looking platforms leading to a door on the other side and numerous vines hang from the ceiling.

Will you attempt to cross using the platforms?

Or...

Try to swing across using the vines, Tarzan style?
